Privacy Policy

Health Check - BMI & More
Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Health Check - BMI & More. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use our health and fitness calculation features.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information that you provide while using the application.

2. Features of the Application

Health Check provides health and fitness calculations, including:

  • BMI (Body Mass Index) Calculator
  • Calorie Calculator
  • Body Fat Calculator
  • Water Intake Calculator
  • Calculation History
  • Health and fitness information
  • Sharing of calculation results
  • Application settings and preferences

3. Information You Enter

Depending on the calculator you use, you may enter information such as age, gender, weight, height, activity level and other values required to perform the selected calculation.

These values are used to calculate the requested health or fitness results.

4. How Your Information Is Stored

Calculation information and application preferences may be stored locally on your device to provide features such as calculation history and settings.

We do not require you to create an account to use the calculation features, and we do not operate a personal health profile for you through the application.

Information stored locally on your device remains under your control. You may use the available history controls within the application to remove saved calculation history.

5. Health and Fitness Information

The application may process health and fitness-related values such as height, weight, age, gender and activity level when you use the relevant calculators.

These values are used to provide calculations and results. The application is not intended to create medical records or provide medical diagnosis.

6. Sharing Results

The application may provide an option to share calculation results using the sharing features available on your device.

When you choose to share a result, the information is shared only through the application or service that you select using the Android sharing system.

7. Advertising

The application may display advertisements provided through third-party advertising services.

Advertising providers may use certain device or advertising information, such as an advertising identifier, in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

We do not use the health calculation values entered into the application to create a personal advertising profile.

The collection and use of information by third-party advertising providers is governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services for purposes such as advertising, application distribution, or other technical functionality.

Third-party service providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party services used by the application.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Deletion

You can remove calculation history using the history management options available in the application.

You may also remove locally stored application data through your device's Android application settings or by uninstalling the application.

11. Children's Privacy

Health Check is intended for users aged 16 and older. The application is not directed toward children under the age of 16 and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

12. Medical Disclaimer

Important: The calculations and information provided by this application are intended for general informational and educational purposes only.

The results are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or medical condition and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice.

Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment decisions.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
